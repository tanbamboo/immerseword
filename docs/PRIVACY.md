# ImmerseWord — Privacy Policy

*Effective: 2026-11-10*

ImmerseWord is a Chrome extension that helps English learners read web pages.
This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## What stays on your device

Everything you do in ImmerseWord is stored **locally in your browser**:

- Your **vocabulary** — the words you marked as known, learning, unknown, or
  ignored, with dates and counts.
- Your **settings** — level, tagging preferences, reader theme and typography.
- Your **dictionary cache** — definitions for words you already looked up
  (so repeat lookups are instant and offline).
- Your **3-pass reading sessions** — kept only until you close the browser.

This data never leaves your device through ImmerseWord. If you have Chrome
Sync enabled, your settings may sync through your own Google account as part
of Chrome's standard extension-settings sync — we have no access to it.

## The one network request we can make

ImmerseWord bundles an offline dictionary for the most frequent English
words. When you look up a word that is **not** in the offline dictionary and
online fallback is enabled, the extension sends that single word to the free
Dictionary API (`api.dictionaryapi.dev`) to fetch its definition. Nothing
else is sent — no page content, no identifiers, no browsing data. You can
turn this off at any time in Settings ("Online dictionary fallback") for a
fully offline extension.

## What we never do

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind.
- No advertising.
- No selling or sharing of data.
- No reading or transmission of your browsing history or page content.

## Data deletion

Everything ImmerseWord stores lives in the extension's browser storage.
Delete it any time via the settings page ("Reset vocabulary", "Clear
dictionary cache"), or remove the extension entirely — which erases all of
its storage.
